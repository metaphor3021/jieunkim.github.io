Visual Silence
2026
discarded paper, hanji, natural lacquer, LED
Wall Light — 123 × 7 × 7 cm
Table Light — 39 × 23 × 8 cm
Visual Silence is a pair of tactile lights that let the user control brightness through touch while keeping the surrounding darkness present.
Project Notes

The project began with my sensitivity to the sudden transition from darkness to light. I became interested not simply in reducing brightness, but in having control over the first level of light encountered after darkness.

Neither light has a visible switch. The user finds the touch point through the material surface and can continuously adjust the brightness by touch. Over time, the hand begins to remember where the control is located.
