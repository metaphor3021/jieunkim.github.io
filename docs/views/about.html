JJieun Kim is an Eindhoven-based artist and designer working with overlooked cellulose waste, particularly newspapers and egg cartons. Her work begins with hands-on material experiments rather than a predetermined form or a standard fabrication method.


She combines paper with mineral binders, natural lacquer and other bio-based materials, building, layering and shaping each piece by hand. Rather than fixing the outcome in advance, she adjusts the work as the materials bind, dry and interact.


Rather than preserving paper’s familiar identity, she is drawn to the unpredictable forms, textures and colours that emerge through these interactions. Her recent work combines organic cellulose fibres with inorganic binders to create mineral-based paper composites, which she develops into objects, sculptures and installations.
